About the HPT Resource Index

The Hypothetical Purchase Task (HPT) is a behavioral-economic method for measuring demand — how much of a commodity people say they would consume across a range of prices. This index catalogs published and preprint HPT studies so researchers can see, at a glance, what has been studied, how, and with which demand models.

What's coded per study

Study

  • Year, journal, DOI, URL
  • Publication status
  • Open-access status

Sample

  • Country, region
  • Population
  • Sample size, mean age

Task

  • Commodity & domain
  • Number of prices, price range
  • Currency

Modeling

  • Demand model, k value
  • Alpha, Q0, breakpoint, Pmax
  • Essential value, R²

Methodology & scope

This is a curated, living index rather than a formal systematic or scoping review. Treat this as ongoing curation, not a claim of exhaustive literature coverage.

What counts as an HPT study. We include studies using the Hypothetical Purchase Task (HPT) methodology specifically — not delay, probability, effort, or social discounting studies, which use a related but distinct framework. HPT studies are usually price-based, but we also include hypothetical budget-allocation studies that substitute a different constraint for price (e.g., likelihood of success rather than a dollar amount), when the underlying demand-curve logic is the same.

How studies are identified. The index began from Jacobs & Bickel's (1999) original HPT paper — the study that introduced the methodology. This is not an exhaustive multi-database systematic search, so coverage will grow over time rather than being complete from the start.

About PROMISE Labs Africa

The HPT Resource Index is a project of PROMISE Labs Africa, a 501(c)(3) scientific nonprofit dedicated to cutting-edge translational behavioral research in Africa — rapidly testing innovative interventions in simulated environments to understand, predict, and influence real-world behaviors.
